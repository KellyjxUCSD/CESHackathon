How should you split the rent?

This choice applies to the apartment’s rent. You’ll split utilities and other monthly fees below.

  • Everyone pays the same. If rent is $2,400 and three people live there, each pays $800.
  • Adjust for each sleeping space. Larger, more private spaces generally cost more. A private bathroom can increase the share, and a living-room spot usually costs less. People sharing a room split that room’s rent equally.
How we calculate room shares

Enter the size of each occupied space and assign everyone a spot. We count 100% of an enclosed room’s area, 80% for an open den or loft, and 70% for a living room. A private bathroom increases that space’s weight by 10%. The preference multiplier lets you adjust its share together. Start at 1 for the suggested split.
